Supplementary Figure 1. Imaging quality control. (A) Number of retained functional runs per participant. (B) Distribution of run mean framewise displacement; the vertical line marks the median. (C) Percentage and count of functional runs retained and excluded from the main imaging set.

Supplementary Figure 2. Whole-brain general linear model summary. Horizontal segments show the signed minimum-to-maximum group z-statistic range in each unthresholded main-analysis map; circles and squares mark the negative and positive extrema, respectively. Dashed vertical lines at |z| = 3.29 are descriptive reference lines only. Passive heat stimulation contained voxels surviving voxelwise false-discovery-rate correction (FDR z threshold = 2.61), whereas none of the regulation-related whole-brain contrasts contained FDR-corrected voxels. Extrema summarize map magnitude and do not localize effects spatially.

Supplementary Figure 3. State-dependence sensitivity across interpretable outcome definitions. Points show z statistics for the direction-by-expected-pain interaction; dashed lines mark |z| = 1.96 and q values show the reported false-discovery-rate correction. The numerically unstable non-cross-fitted fractional-logit model is omitted from the figure and remains documented in Supplementary Table S3.

Supplementary Figure 4. Robustness of principal negative regional effects. Points show the number of analysis sets—main, stricter run exclusion and high-motion participant exclusion—in which each down-minus-passive regional estimate was negative and false-discovery-rate significant.

Supplementary Figure 5. NPS estimation-path and corrected trial-wise diagnostics. (A) Contrast-map NPS group means in the full sample and after excluding the influential participant; connected points show the same contrast under the two analysis sets. (B) The corresponding contrast-map NPS values for the excluded influential participant, shown on a separate scale to make leverage visible without compressing the group means. (C) One-sample t statistics for the four primary rating-adjusted FWL-LSS regulation contrasts; dashed lines mark |t| = 1.96 and q values use the reported FDR correction by measure. (D) The passive temperature slope from the same rating-adjusted FWL-LSS analysis, shown separately because its large t statistic would otherwise compress the regulation contrasts. The temperature result confirms strong thermal sensitivity, whereas no regulation contrast was reliable.

Supplementary Figure 6. AR(1)-prewhitened lagged-coupling sensitivity and exploratory DCM-family results. (A) Correlations between up-regulation success and the AR(1)-prewhitened forward, reverse-direction, synchronous zero-lag and time-reversed composites, with descriptive Fisher-z 95% intervals. (B) Shared-source permutation and nonzero within-run circular-shift null distributions for the AR(1)-prewhitened forward association; the vertical line marks the observed correlation. (C) Participant-bootstrap differences between the AR(1)-prewhitened forward association and matched controls; q values use Benjamini-Hochberg FDR correction across the three comparisons. (D) Selected retained or substantively relevant DCM-family parameters with 90% posterior credible intervals and posterior probabilities. Coupling follow-ups remain conditional on the selected pathway; DCM effects are exploratory model-family sensitivities.
