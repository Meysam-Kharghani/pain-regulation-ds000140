Figure 1. Behavioral validation of voluntary pain regulation. (A) Adjusted instruction effects on standardized ratings relative to passive trials; points show model estimates and whiskers show 95% confidence intervals, with zero indicating no instruction effect. (B) Participant-level direction-corrected standardized regulation success. Lines connect the same participant across up- and down-regulation; diamonds and whiskers show group means and bootstrap 95% confidence intervals. (C) Participant-level up-minus-down success differences. The violin summarizes the distribution, points show all 33 participants, and the diamond and whisker show the group mean and bootstrap 95% confidence interval. Both directions showed positive mean regulation success, whereas the mean directional difference was small and not reliable.

Figure 2. Sensitivity of the behavioral state-dependence result to outcome definition and change-score coupling. (A) z statistics for the direction-by-expected-pain interaction across alternative outcome definitions; dashed reference lines mark |z| = 1.96. Only the standardized-success definition survived the reported false-discovery-rate correction across the tested contrast family. (B) Simulated null distributions for the raw up-minus-down slope under unclipped and [0, 200]-clipped rating simulations. Violins show the null distributions, squares and whiskers show null medians and central 95% intervals, and the vertical line marks the observed raw slope. Under the unclipped null the observed value was close to the center of the distribution, whereas under the clipped null it lay beyond the simulated distribution.

Figure 3. Regional down-regulation effects and run-position sensitivity. (A) Down-minus-passive effects for regions surviving false-discovery-rate correction across the 32-ROI family; points show mean effects and whiskers show 95% bootstrap confidence intervals. Negative values indicate lower regional responses during down-regulation, while the positive left dlPFC/IFJ estimate indicates greater activity. (B) Run-matched down-minus-passive sensitivity estimates for left S2/operculum and right posterior insula using nearest-passive, adjacent-mean-passive and following-passive comparators. Whiskers are 95% t intervals from participant-level paired differences. Nearest- and adjacent-passive estimates were nominally negative, but none of these selected-ROI comparisons survived the corresponding 32-ROI FDR correction. Panel A uses run-wise mean-scaled GLM contrast units, whereas Panel B uses native BOLD-intensity FWL-LSS units; magnitudes should not be compared directly across panels.

Figure 4. Rating-adjusted exploratory lagged prefrontal-valuation coupling, matched controls and transition-level sensitivity. (A) Correlations between up-regulation success and the selected forward composite, reverse-direction, synchronous zero-lag and time-reversed formulations, with descriptive Fisher-z 95% intervals. (B) Shared-source permutation and nonzero within-run circular-shift null distributions; the vertical line marks the observed forward correlation. (C) Participant-bootstrap differences between the forward association and matched controls; the forward formulation was more negative than each matched control in the reported bootstrap comparisons, with FDR correction applied across the three contrasts. (D) Covariate-adjusted transition-level sensitivity using all 297 up-regulation transitions from the archived rating-adjusted AR(1)-prewhitened trial estimates. Points show success-by-lagged-source interaction coefficients for vmPFC, mOFC, the combined vmPFC/mOFC target and their equal-weight three-target mean; whiskers are participant-cluster bootstrap 95% intervals. All coupling follow-ups remain conditional on the same-sample selected pathway and do not constitute independent validation.
