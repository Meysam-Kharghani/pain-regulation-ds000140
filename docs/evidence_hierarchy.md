# Evidence hierarchy

| Level | Result | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Primary behavioral | Up- and down-regulation shifted ratings in the instructed direction | Task validation |
| Primary regional imaging | Down-minus-passive decreases in bilateral S2/operculum and insular regions; increase in left dlPFC/IFJ | Condition-level regional association |
| Boundary | No regulation contrast survived whole-brain voxelwise FDR | No spatially unrestricted regulation claim |
| Method-sensitive | NPS reduction in contrast maps did not reproduce in rating-adjusted FWL-LSS regulation contrasts | Estimation-path dependence |
| Exploratory individual difference | Rating-adjusted forward dlPFC/IFJ-to-vmPFC/mOFC composite correlated with up-regulation success | Post-selection association |
| Matched controls | Reverse, synchronous, and time-reversed controls were weaker; two conditional null models bounded the forward association | Temporal and shared-source characterization |
| Model-based sensitivity | Six run-separated DCM families did not show a stable family-wide up-specific mechanism | No convergent causal validation |

Regional co-occurrence does not establish top-down suppression. Lagged coupling is predictive precedence rather than neuronal causality. Condition-average and individual-difference results address different inferential levels and are not evidence for two distinct mechanisms by themselves.
